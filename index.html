Patch 7.42

Dota 2 gameplay update, 18 November 2026. Changes are sorted by how much they alter the way you play.

Tier 1: every game Tier 2: big systems Tier 3: balance Tier 4: comfort and fixes

Tier 1Changes how every game flows

Sturdier heroes, flatter economy, weaker spells, and a much earlier Tormentor.

200base hero health, up from 120. Health per Strength drops 22 → 20.
90/minpassive gold, flat all game. It used to climb to 111.
50/minpassive XP for every hero, even while dead. Level 30 now needs 66,600 XP.
15:00Tormentor spawn, down from 20:00. Always drops Greater Horn of Battle.
5 minRoshan’s respawn floor. The timer shrinks by 1 minute per kill.
20%default Item Potency for illusions. They can now apply most on-hit item effects.
Spell power is cut. Items add less spell amp, area and cast range. Most magic and pure damage is lower, and some long slows are shorter. Magic resistance per Intelligence rises to 0.125%. Tormentor’s second death also drops an Aghanim’s Shard.

Tier 2Big systems and reworks

New items, a rebuilt neutral system, a new jungle layout, and a lot of reworked heroes.

Items

  • Six new: Bloodslinger, Caduceus, Ristul’s Devotion, Sabatons of Mordiggian, Skypiercer, Starforge
  • Returns: Quarterstaff, at 900 gold
  • Removed: Javelin Khanda Helm of the Overlord
  • Recipes shift: Butterfly Monkey King Bar Bloodthorn Maelstrom Mask of Madness
  • Cheaper by 100 gold: Kaya Sange Yasha

Neutral items and jungle

  • 4 tiers instead of 5, unlocking at 0, 15, 30 and 50 minutes
  • Fixed pools: every offering is set at game start and visible in the shop
  • 12 new artifacts, 13 removed, enchantments reworked
  • Home jungles: most camps now spawn on one side only
  • New camps: Radigull, Odobenus, Eimermole, Ancient Ogre Seal
  • Map: Twin Gates move to the rims, and mid towers shift to equalize base distances
Hero reworks, A to M
  • Juggernaut loses Swiftslash
  • Meepo becomes Universal, MegaMeepo is gone
  • Beastmaster loses Drums of Slom, gets a Thunderhide with Scepter
  • Chen gets a reworked Zealot
  • Leshrac gets a reworked Nihilism
  • Chaos Knight, Lycan get new innates
Hero reworks, N to Z
  • Nature’s Prophet: Curse of the Oldgrowth is now a stacking attack debuff
  • Nyx Assassin: loses Mana Burn, gains Scarab Metabolism
  • Pugna: Life Drain overhauled, Scepter gives ability recasts
  • Undying: Shard grants Zombie Bite
  • Underlord: Invading Force gives a barrier
  • Phantom Assassin: Blur reworked for invisibility
  • Tinker: Warp Flare slows instead of rooting
  • Venomancer: Noxious Plague can’t be dispelled
  • Shadow Shaman: always 3 chickens
  • Morphling, Weaver, Sand King: new Scepter upgrades

Tier 3Balance and mechanics

Rule tweaks across lanes and spells, plus the hero buffs and nerfs worth knowing.

  • Lanes: pulled creeps are slowed up to 25%, and late-game siege creeps spawn more often
  • Glyph: 2 charges, 6-minute replenish
  • Fountain True Sight radius: 1200 → 1800
  • Break: many innates are now fully or partly disabled by it
  • Spell lifesteal from duplicate items now stacks
  • Smoke of Deceit: stock 4, 330s restock, +25% speed for 50s
  • Invisibility Rune also grants a Smoke-style disguise
  • Couriers and several summons get fragile health pips

Tier 4Comfort and fixes

Mostly Steam-post items: controls, performance, and closed exploits.

  • Hotkeys for Invoker’s spells, Largo’s songs and Kez’s Sais
  • Unit hotkeys for Lycan’s wolves and several neutral creeps
  • Dota+: Hand of Midas gets its own gold category
  • Performance: smoother illusion spawning, lighter server load
  • Exploits closed: Shard sell-on-apply, stash item transfers, Beastmaster creep buff, Meepo infinite gold
  • Crash fixed: selling all Teleport Scrolls during a clone teleport

General updates and N to Z heroes were checked against the official notes. Items, neutral items and A to M details come from a third-party breakdown. Hero kill bounty is “rescaled” in the official file, with no formula given. Icons are original drawings made for this page, not Valve artwork.
